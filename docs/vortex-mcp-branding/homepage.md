# Vortex MCP

[logo: vortex-logo-square-480.png]

**Connect your AI assistant to Google Ads and Google Drive.**

Vortex MCP is a connector service from PT Vortex Buana Edumedia. It lets AI assistants such as Claude Desktop work with your Google Ads accounts and Google Drive files through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The assistant acts only when you ask, using the permissions you approve on Google's consent screen.

## What you can do

**Google Ads**
- List the Google Ads accounts you can access.
- Ask about campaigns, budgets and performance, and get reports.
- With the write-enabled connector: create ad assets and manage labels, after confirming each change.

**Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides**
- Search and read your files and folders.
- Summarise, draft and edit Google Docs, Sheets and Slides.
- Create, move, rename, share and organise files when you ask.

## Why Vortex MCP asks for access to your Google data

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Email address (and, for Google Ads, name and profile picture) | Identify which Google Account is connected |
| Google Ads | Run the reports you ask for; in the write-enabled connector, create assets and manage labels you request |
| Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides | Find, read, create and edit the files you ask the assistant to work with |

Vortex MCP does not keep copies of your Google Ads data or Drive files, does not sell your data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Vortex MCP's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## How to connect

1. In your AI assistant, add the Vortex MCP connector from your Vortex administrator.
2. Sign in with Google and review the permissions.
3. Ask your assistant to work with your Google Ads or Drive data.

You can remove access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party connections.
